#
# With `channel = "stable"` rustup re-resolves on every invocation and will
# auto-update whenever a new stable ships. On 2026-08-05 that broke the iad-ci
# `rust-verify` gate for every repo using it: the builder tried to sync stable
# 1.97.1 (released 2026-07-16) mid-job and died with
