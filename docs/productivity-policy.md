## Deliver useful outcomes

- Work must resolve a selected product outcome, demonstrated defect, operational
  risk, or concrete decision. State the benefit and observable completion condition
  on the owning bead. Commit count, bead count, coverage percentage, spare quota,
  and an empty queue do not justify work.
- Keep implementation, necessary tests, documentation, verification, and delivery
  together under one outcome owner. Split only independent deliverables or real
  dependencies. Do not create children solely to rerun checks or record evidence.
- Generated discoveries are proposals until explicitly admitted against those
  criteria. Keep `proposal-pending` and `human` on unapproved proposals. Do not
  remove these labels, generate alternatives to evade them, or approve your own
  proposed work. An exhausted approved queue may idle.
- Record status and verification evidence on the bead. Never manufacture edits,
  commits, reports, or repeated close attempts to demonstrate activity. Preserve
  unrelated changes, including another worker's staged files.
- Run required checks and checks that address a concrete risk. After they pass,
  repeat them only when relevant inputs change or a specific failure requires it.
  Reuse only independently verified evidence with matching inputs; never invent a
  cache hit or bypass a required gate. If infrastructure prevents verification,
  retain the implementation and record the exact failed step for recovery.
- Judge progress by accepted outcomes, their time and cost, repeated attempts,
  and regressions. Finish selected work before opening speculative work. Unused
  capacity is preferable to creating an unjustified maintenance burden.
